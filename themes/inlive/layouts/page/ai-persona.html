Introducing Inlive AI Persona

In today's technology-driven world, the quest for more natural and intuitive communication with digital systems is becoming increasingly important. At Inlive, we are dedicated to bridging the gap between cutting-edge technology and user-friendly experiences.

Inlive AI Persona transforms everyday devices into sensory tools that enhance human-like interaction capabilities. By integrating webcams, microphones, speakers, and video displays, AI agents can simulate human senses:

Utilizing Devices as AI Senses

  • Camera acts as the "eyes" for AI, allowing it to capture visual data and detect changes or expressions, enriching the interactive experience.

  • Microphone functions as "ears," enabling the AI to listen to users, understand their spoken language, and capture nuances in communication.

  • Speakers serves as the "mouth," letting the AI convey information and express itself audibly to create a more engaging dialog.

  • Video display provides a "face" or interface through which users can engage visually, enhancing presence and relatability in interactions.

Use case

  • Virtual Receptionist

    Imagine walking into an office, hotel, or corporate headquarters and being greeted instantly—not by a person, but by a virtual receptionist powered by AI. Using just its microphone and speakers, it listens to your questions, understands your needs, and responds with helpful information.

    Whether you're checking in for an appointment, asking for directions, or inquiring about services, the virtual receptionist delivers clear, conversational responses. It can even display essential details, like schedules or maps, on a screen only when needed, keeping interactions simple and focused.

    With an adaptive tone and natural voice, this AI-powered assistant ensures every interaction feels warm, professional, and efficient. It’s a smarter, more accessible way to keep your visitors informed and delighted from the moment they arrive.

  • Virtual Hiring Recruiter

    Imagine having a hiring recruiter that’s always ready to conduct interviews, no matter the time or place. With AI for virtual recruitment, your candidates can connect online and experience a seamless, professional interview process.

    Using voice and natural conversation, the virtual recruiter asks thoughtful questions, gathers all the essential details, and records responses accurately. It evaluates candidates based solely on their qualifications and responses, free from biases related to appearance. Whether it’s qualifications, past experiences, or role-specific scenarios, every aspect is covered with precision. Key information is summarized and organized for your review, saving you hours of manual effort.

    By creating a consistent and stress-free experience for every candidate, it simplifies your hiring process and helps you find the right talent efficiently.

  • Virtual Call Center Officer

    Imagine a call center officer who’s always available to assist your customers, day or night. With an AI-powered virtual support system, it can answer questions, resolve common issues, and even create detailed follow-up tickets for more complex problems.

    Powered by your company’s knowledge base, the virtual officer delivers accurate, helpful information tailored to each customer’s needs. It guides them step-by-step through solutions, ensuring clarity and confidence in resolving their concerns. For inquiries that require further attention, it generates comprehensive report tickets, providing your team with everything needed to follow up efficiently.

    With natural, conversational interactions and consistent performance, this virtual officer offers reliable, high-quality support. It enhances customer satisfaction, reduces wait times, and streamlines operations, making it a valuable addition to any customer service team.
